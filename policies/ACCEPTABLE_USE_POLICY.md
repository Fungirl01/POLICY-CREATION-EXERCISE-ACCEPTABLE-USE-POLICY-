1. Purpose
-------------
Maple Health Connect provides a secure messaging and scheduling platform for clinics and their patients. Every time someone uses company equipment, networks, or accounts, they affect the security of patient data. This policy sets clear rules for how our technology should and should not be used. It protects our patients, our clinics, and our reputation.

2. Scope
---------
This policy applies to:
- All Maple Health Connect employees (full-time, part-time, temporary)
- Contractors, consultants, and interns
- Any third-party accessing Maple Health Connect systems (e.g., clinic admin users are covered by their own agreements, but we hold ourselves to a higher standard)

Covers all company resources:
- Laptops, desktops, phones, tablets
- Network access (Wi Fi, VPN)
- Software, cloud accounts (Office 365, Slack, GitHub, etc.)
- Email, internal chat, file storage (OneDrive/SharePoint)
- Any personal device used for work (BYOD – if approved)

3. Expectations
----------------
Acceptable Use
- Use company resources for legitimate work purposes, primarily supporting clinics and maintaining our platform.
- Lock your screen when stepping away (Windows: Windows + L, Mac: Ctrl + Cmd + Q).
- Use strong, unique passwords stored in the company password manager.
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately (see Incident Reporting Policy).
- Use the corporate VPN when working remotely or on public Wi Fi.
- Connect only approved peripherals (company-issued mouse, keyboard, headset).
- Store work files in approved company cloud locations (OneDrive/SharePoint), not local desktop.

Prohibited Use

Security & Data Handling
- Share your login credentials with anyone, not even IT (IT will never ask).
- Leave patient data visible on your screen in public places (coffee shops, airplanes, trains).
- Download PHI to personal devices or USB drives.
- Forward work emails to personal email accounts.
- Disable or bypass security software (antivirus, endpoint detection, VPN).

Personal Activities
- Use company devices for illegal activities (piracy, hacking, downloading copyrighted content).
- Access adult content, gambling sites, or violent material.
- Run a personal business or side hustle on company time/equipment.
- Install unapproved software (including freeware, screensavers, or browser extensions not vetted by IT).

System Integrity
- Attempt to bypass network restrictions or firewalls.
- Install remote access tools (TeamViewer, AnyDesk) without approval.
- Connect unauthorized personal devices to the corporate network.
- Remove or tamper with security labels, asset tags, or disk encryption.

Email & Communication
- Send mass marketing emails or spam.
- Impersonate another employee or clinic user.
- Use company email for personal, political, or religious advocacy.
- Click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments, even if it looks like it's from a coworker.

4. Reporting Requirements
--------------------------
If you see someone violating this policy (or accidentally violate it yourself):

1) For accidental violations (e.g., you clicked a phishing link, installed something without thinking):
- Report within 1 hour to incident@maplehealthconnect.com or via the Internal IT Portal.
- You will not be punished for honest mistakes but hiding them will be treated seriously.

2) For intentional violations by others (e.g., a coworker sharing their password, downloading patient data to a personal drive):
- Report to your manager or the Security Lead directly (Slack DM or email).
- You can also report anonymously via our Ethics Hotline (link on intranet).

3) For violations that put patient data at immediate risk (e.g., a lost laptop with PHI):
- Follow the Critical Incident process from the Incident Reporting Policy (call hotline immediately).

Consequences of violation:
- Accidental violation reported promptly → coaching and possibly additional training.
- Negligent or repeated violations → written warning.
- Intentional violation, hiding a violation, or accessing patient data without a work need → termination and possible legal action.

5. Exceptions & Approvals
--------------------------
If you need to use company resources in a way that would otherwise violate this policy (e.g., a developer needs to temporarily disable antivirus for testing), you must get written approval from your manager and the Security & Compliance Lead. Keep that approval in a ticket. No exceptions without documentation.

6. Policy Compliance & Acknowledgment
--------------------------------------
All employees must read, sign, and return an acknowledgment form (attached) within 7 days of hire and annually thereafter. Contractors must sign before receiving any system access. Failure to sign does not excuse compliance; you are still bound by this policy.

7. Review & Updates
--------------------
This policy will be reviewed every 12 months or after any major incident. Suggestions for improvement are welcome: email compliance@maplehealthconnect.com.
